About the Researcher

Amzal Mortin Andas is a lecturer and researcher at Universitas Bani Saleh. He holds a Ph.D. in Nursing from Lincoln University College, Malaysia. His research interests include gerontology nursing, elderly mental health, and evidence-based chronic care.

His work moves across the full arc of scholarly contribution — from peer-reviewed research on loneliness, depression, sarcopenia, and quality of life in older adults, to academic books, registered intellectual property, and digital learning platforms for nursing students. He serves as a peer reviewer for a range of Scopus-indexed international journals.

His ambition is clear: to build internationally oriented, evidence-based nursing science that improves the lives of older adults — and to translate that evidence into education that reaches every nursing student.
